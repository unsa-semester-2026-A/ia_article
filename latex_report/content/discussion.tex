\subsection{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The revised evaluation establishes the importance of adaptation to the target traffic domain. Macro AP-rIoU is 0.0784 for the zero-shot configuration, while the five fine-tuned runs fall between 0.6519 and 0.6917. Such a difference indicates that a limited mapping from pretrained categories cannot adequately represent the nine SMART labels, the local camera geometry, the congestion patterns, or the orientation of vehicles in these scenes.

Although Base 1 has the highest overall score, Base 2 records both the largest AP@0.80 and the greatest number of true positives. The latter therefore localizes part of the prediction set more precisely, but this benefit is accompanied by a sharp increase in spurious detections: false positives rise from 62,994 to 165,974. The additional detections reduce the aggregate ranking quality enough for Base 2 to finish slightly below Base 1. This comparison illustrates that a stronger augmentation policy can improve one operating characteristic without improving the class-balanced metric as a whole.

Neither segmentation-assisted condition overtakes the raw-image baselines. Improvement B reaches 0.6550 Macro AP-rIoU, whereas adding LaMa-corrected pixels in Improvement C raises the value to 0.6682. Improvement C also produces fewer false positives than Improvements A and B and achieves 98.93 FPS. The combined treatment therefore yields the best proposed configuration and the most efficient evaluation run, but its synthetic training increment does not supply enough localization or ranking benefit to close the gap with Base 1 or Base 2.

This result is compatible with prior work showing that plausible appearance and correct labels do not, by themselves, guarantee gains from synthetic augmentation~\cite{Weber2021ArtificialImagesVehicleDetection,Mumuni2024,HuangW2025,Alimisis2025Advances,Benkedadra2024,ZhuH2025}. Pasted objects can change the joint distribution of context, scale, overlap, density, and category frequency. Consequently, masks and oriented boxes may remain geometrically valid even when the resulting examples are less informative than naturally occurring traffic scenes. A controlled ablation is therefore necessary before attributing an accuracy benefit to additional generated data.

The category results also show that sample frequency is only one source of difficulty. Motocicleta has the lowest score in every trained condition, but Articulado attains very high AP despite being less common. Differences in projected size, visual diversity, partial occlusion, and orientation consistency are likely to contribute alongside imbalance~\cite{Espinosa2020,Cheng2023SmallObjectSurvey,Nikouei2025SmallObjectSurvey}. Future sampling policies should consequently respond to measured error modes instead of assigning the same insertion strategy to every class.

From a deployment perspective, all fine-tuned models process between 83.76 and 98.93 frames per second on the reported hardware. The condition with maximum speed is not the one with maximum accuracy: Improvement C leads throughput, whereas Base 1 leads Macro AP-rIoU. Model selection should therefore depend on the operational cost of missed and false detections and on whether the additional processing margin is actually required.

The central outcome is methodological rather than a claim of superiority for synthetic data. The experiment maintains a real, fixed validation partition and subjects six training conditions to one rotated-box evaluator. Under those controls, the tested SAM-assisted variants provide measurable efficiency and internal improvements but fail to exceed the strongest conventional baseline. Reporting this negative comparison narrows the design space for subsequent augmentation studies and prevents performance claims based solely on the number or appearance of generated samples.
