\section{Results and Discussion}
\label{sec:results_discussion}

\subsection{Results}
\label{sec:results}

The ablation reported here is derived from the final evaluation packages of Base 0, Base 1, Base 2, and Improvements A--C. To ensure comparability, every package is processed with an identical validation split, taxonomy, homography-based trajectory filter, and Macro AP-rIoU calculation. After filtering, prediction coordinates are multiplied by 3.0 for metric computation; the filtering stage itself continues to operate in the original coordinate space.

Evaluation covers 10,873 frames containing 109,448 annotated vehicles. The sequence shown in Figure~\ref{fig:final_evaluation_flow} is applied without variation to all six runs. Accordingly, the reported comparison is based on the final class-sensitive rotated-box criterion rather than on validation indicators produced internally during training.

\subsubsection{Dataset and Augmentation Artifacts}
\label{sec:dataset_characterization_results}

Inspection of the preprocessing outputs identified 54,262 training frames from 1,088 clips and 601,934 oriented annotations. The held-out partition consists of 10,873 real frames with 109,448 labeled objects. None of the scores therefore depends on augmented or resampled validation imagery.

The training augmentation package, identified as SAM CP production v1, adds 1,501 generated images, each paired with a YOLO-OBB annotation file, and includes 3,391 pasted vehicle instances. The validation directory receives no generated samples. Its principal characteristics are listed in Table~\ref{tab:augmentation_release_summary}.

\subsubsection{Final Macro AP-rIoU Comparison}
\label{sec:final_macro_ap_results}

The corrected measurements are reported in Table~\ref{tab:ablation_final_metrics} and Figure~\ref{fig:ablation_macro_metrics}. Fine-tuning on the target data markedly raises performance relative to the zero-shot setting. Base 1 ranks first with a Macro AP-rIoU of 0.6917, whereas Base 2 reaches 0.6892. The absolute gap is only 0.0025 (0.37\% of the Base 1 score), but the more aggressive conventional augmentation used by Base 2 does not increase the overall metric.

Improvement C leads the three proposed data modifications, with 0.6682 Macro AP-rIoU, 0.7519 at rIoU 0.50, and 0.4935 at rIoU 0.80. Its aggregate score is 0.0132 higher than Improvement B and 0.0163 higher than Improvement A. Even so, the remaining deficits are 0.0235 relative to Base 1 and 0.0210 relative to Base 2. Thus, under this protocol, SAM-guided copy-paste does not surpass training performed on the unmodified image set.

\subsubsection{Operational and Motion-Filter Diagnostics}
\label{sec:operational_diagnostics_results}

The operational statistics expose a trade-off between detection quality and throughput-related behavior. Base 1 couples the top Macro AP-rIoU with only 62,994 false positives. Compared with this run, Base 2 finds 818 additional true objects and misses 818 fewer objects, but it also introduces 102,980 more false detections. This imbalance is consistent with Base 2's advantage at rIoU 0.80 and its slightly inferior aggregate score.

Within the proposed group, Improvement C provides the most favorable runtime and error profile. Its false-positive total is 34.4\% lower than that of Improvement A and 34.4\% lower than that of Improvement B; its Macro AP-rIoU is higher by 2.50\% and 2.01\%, respectively. It also records the maximum throughput, 98.93 FPS. These gains, however, do not compensate for the accuracy difference with the two raw-image baselines.

\subsubsection{Class-Level Performance}
\label{sec:class_error_distribution_results}

Class-wise mean AP-rIoU is displayed in Figure~\ref{fig:class_fp_by_condition}. Base 1 leads for Auto, Microbus, Mototaxi, and Motocicleta, and attains 1.000 for Articulado. Base 2 is superior for Combi, Minibus, Omnibus, and Camion while remaining close on the other labels. Improvement C ranks first among the proposed variants for six classes---Auto, Combi, Microbus, Omnibus, Articulado, and Mototaxi---yet these class-specific gains do not overturn the baseline ranking.

For every fine-tuned model, Motocicleta has the lowest mean AP-rIoU, ranging from 0.2205 to 0.2921. Its behavior is compatible with both low representation and the difficulty of detecting small targets. Base 0 yields nonzero values only for Auto and Camion, since the adopted DOTA-to-SMART mapping contains those categories but cannot represent all nine target classes.

\subsubsection{Improvement B Internal Training Evidence}
\label{sec:improvement_b_training_results}

Improvement B stopped after 12 of the planned 40 epochs under patience 5, batch size 96, 640-pixel inputs, AdamW optimization, mixed precision, and two Tesla T4 devices. During training, the highest Ultralytics mAP50 was 0.929 at epoch 11, whereas the maximum mAP50--95 was 0.761 at epoch 7. At the final logged epoch, precision was 0.900, recall 0.859, mAP50 0.928, and mAP50--95 0.757.

\begin{figure}[!t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{latex_report/src/fig/mejora_b_training_curves.png}
  \caption{Ultralytics training-validation history for Improvement B; its score under the common final evaluator is 0.6550 Macro AP-rIoU.}
  \label{fig:mejora_b_training_curves}
\end{figure}

Training-time indicators and the final scores should not be interpreted as equivalent because their aggregation rules and evaluation stages differ. They nevertheless show that optimization completed normally and that the detector generated usable predictions. For the comparative analysis, the final evaluator is authoritative because it enforces the target class IDs, rotated-IoU thresholds, motion filtering, and identical scoring logic across the six artifacts.
