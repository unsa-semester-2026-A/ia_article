\section{Results and Discussion}
\label{sec:results_discussion}

\subsection{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This section reports the available evidence as an ablation study. The current artifact set contains final Macro AP-rIoU packages for Base 0, Base 2, Improvement A, and Improvement C; a consolidated final-metrics JSON for Base 1; and Ultralytics training artifacts for Improvement B. The final Macro AP-rIoU package for Improvement B is not available, so its evidence is reported separately as training-level validation rather than merged into the final-evaluator comparison.

\subsubsection{Dataset and Augmentation Artifacts}
\label{sec:dataset_characterization_results}

The preprocessing audit confirms that the training CSV contains 54,262 frames, 1,088 clips, and 601,934 OBB annotations. The validation subset used by the final evaluator contains 10,873 frames and 109,448 ground-truth instances. This confirms that the reported final metrics operate on a fixed validation partition rather than on synthetic or resampled validation data.

The train-only augmentation release, named SAM CP production v1, contains 1,501 synthetic images and 1,501 matching YOLO-OBB label files, with 3,391 inserted vehicle instances. No validation images or validation labels are generated. The same synthetic delta is intended for Improvement B over raw training images and for Improvement C over LaMa-corrected training images. Table~\ref{tab:augmentation_release_summary} summarizes the generated augmentation set.

The validation checks cover the main failure modes that could compromise the experiment. They verify image-label stem equality, reject empty deltas, check every generated YOLO-OBB label for nine fields and normalized coordinates, confirm that no synthetic file shadows a real training frame, and ensure that every synthetic background belongs to the train split. These checks establish that the augmentation set is suitable for ablation, although they do not by themselves prove a detector-performance gain.

\subsubsection{Final Macro AP-rIoU Comparison}
\label{sec:final_macro_ap_results}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation_final_metrics} and Figure~\ref{fig:ablation_macro_metrics} show the final evaluator results available at this stage. Base 1 is the only condition with non-zero Macro AP-rIoU, reaching 0.577 over rIoU thresholds 0.50--0.80, with 0.666 at rIoU 0.50 and 0.411 at rIoU 0.80. The count snapshot at rIoU 0.50 also shows 79,098 true positives, 183,084 false positives, and 30,350 false negatives for Base 1.

Base 0, Base 2, Improvement A, and Improvement C produce candidate detections, but none are accepted as true positives by the final class-and-geometry matching step after motion filtering. Their final AP values are therefore 0.000 across the three reported summary metrics. This result means that the uploaded final-evaluation artifacts do not support a positive effect for zero-shot inference, classical online augmentation, LaMa-only training, or the LaMa plus SAM copy-paste condition. It also indicates that Base 2, Improvement A, and Improvement C should be audited for checkpoint, class-remapping, OBB-coordinate, or evaluation-compatibility issues before their zero scores are interpreted as purely visual-model failures.

Improvement C does reduce the post-filter candidate volume relative to Base 2 and Improvement A, with 201,344 false positives at rIoU 0.50 compared with 252,890 and 253,713, respectively. It also records the highest throughput among the final ZIP packages, at 98.9 FPS. However, this operational improvement does not translate into accepted detections under the final metric because the true-positive count remains zero.

\subsubsection{Base 1 Class-Level Behavior}
\label{sec:base1_class_results}

Because Base 1 is the only condition with a non-zero final score, its class-level profile provides the most useful diagnostic view of the current detector behavior. Figure~\ref{fig:base1_class_ap} shows that the strongest classes are Microbus (0.851), Omnibus (0.701), Mototaxi (0.627), and Auto (0.626). The weakest classes are Motocicleta (0.275) and Articulado (0.406). This pattern shows that the difficulty is not explained by class frequency alone. Some minority classes remain measurable, while classes with small visual footprints, ambiguous orientation, or sparse examples still limit the macro-averaged score.

\begin{figure*}[!t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.88\textwidth]{latex_report/src/fig/base1_class_ap.png}
  \caption{Class-wise AP-rIoU for Base 1 over thresholds 0.50--0.80. Base 1 is the current reference condition because it is the only uploaded final-result artifact with non-zero true positives.}
  \label{fig:base1_class_ap}
\end{figure*}

The threshold-level counts for Base 1 also show the expected localization penalty as the rIoU threshold increases. At rIoU 0.50, precision is 0.302, recall is 0.723, and F1 is 0.426. At rIoU 0.80, precision decreases to 0.227, recall to 0.545, and F1 to 0.321. Since inference begins with a low confidence threshold of 0.001 to preserve candidates for AP computation, the false-positive count is high; the relevant comparison is therefore the ranked AP and the consistent validation protocol rather than the raw candidate count alone.

\subsubsection{Improvement B Training Evidence}
\label{sec:improvement_b_training_results}

Improvement B has training artifacts but not a final Macro AP-rIoU evaluation ZIP. The uploaded run completed 12 epochs of a requested 40-epoch schedule with patience 5, batch size 96, image size 640, AdamW, automatic mixed precision, and two Tesla T4 GPUs. The best internal YOLO validation mAP50 is 0.929 at epoch 11, and the best internal mAP50-95 is 0.761 at epoch 7. The last recorded epoch reports precision 0.900, recall 0.859, mAP50 0.928, and mAP50-95 0.757.

\begin{figure*}[!t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.88\textwidth]{latex_report/src/fig/mejora_b_training_curves.png}
  \caption{Internal Ultralytics validation curves for Improvement B. These values show that the run converged, but they are not a substitute for the final Macro AP-rIoU evaluation with motion filtering.}
  \label{fig:mejora_b_training_curves}
\end{figure*}

These Improvement B metrics are encouraging as training diagnostics, but they are not directly comparable with Table~\ref{tab:ablation_final_metrics}. Ultralytics mAP uses the model's validation loop, while the paper's final metric uses the SMART validation frames, official class identifiers, rotated IoU thresholds, and the homography-based motion filter. Therefore, the raw-pixel SAM copy-paste condition cannot be assigned a final Macro AP-rIoU effect from the current artifacts. The LaMa plus SAM copy-paste condition is no longer pending: its uploaded final package is included as Improvement C and obtains 0.000 under the final evaluator.
