\section*{Data availability}
\label{sec:data_availability}
The dataset used for the experiments is available through the official MTC distribution hosted on Kaggle at \href{https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/mtc-smart-challenge-ia-para-la-movilidad-del-peru/data}{SMART Challenge 2026 data} after acceptance of the applicable platform and release terms~\cite{MTC2026SmartChallenge}. The raw archives are not redistributed with this repository.
